Glow is for live events. People can only join a room while they're physically inside the event venue: the app takes one precise location fix when you join, the server checks it against the room's area, and only pass or fail is kept. Coordinates are never stored.

Signing in: use Sign in with Apple, or the 6-digit code Glow emails you. No demo account is needed, and any account works.

Without being at a venue you can sign in, finish onboarding (first name, birthday, who you want to see, and 1 to 3 profile photos), allow notifications, and open the room scanner and room code entry. Joining a room will say you're not in the room yet. That's expected outside a venue, and nothing is shown about who is in any room.

Privacy: outfit photos have every face blurred on the iPhone (Vision face detection) before upload. Cards never show faces, names or distances. A tap is never revealed unless it's mutual. Glow is 18+; onboarding refuses younger birthdays.

Export compliance: Glow uses only standard HTTPS encryption (ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption is false).
