# X2 · Blind mode in the iPhone app

Branch `blind/x2-ios` (from `blind-mode`, with `blind-mode` merged in: X1's contract and U1/U3 design work).

## Built

- **AppCore** (X2-owned files): `Services/BlindService.swift` (protocol, models, `BlindChatID`/`BlindCardID`, `BlindError`, the fixed numbers) and `Mocks/PreviewBlindService.swift` (an actor over a value-type `BlindWorld` that follows the real rules: 20 right swipes per UTC day, a drop opens from 5 right swipes and at most 5 chats, chats only for mutual right swipes and only after a drop, a one-sided reveal stays `open`, the partner only on a revealed chat, the guess pool is my own right swipes, a block removes the chat, leaving Blind deletes swipes, matches and chats). Generated portraits only, no real people.
- **Routes**: `.blind`, `.blindDrop`, `.blindChat(BlindChatID)`, `.blindReveal`, `.blindGuess`, `.blindReport`; `AppTab.blind`; `Router.blindPath`. A fourth tab, Blind (Rooms, Matches, Blind, Settings). `DebugRoute` takes `blind`, `blindDrop`, `blindChat/<id>` and the rest.
- **FeatureBlind** (new GlowKit module, same dependencies as other features): opt-in; deck (drag and flick, stamps, buttons, cap banner with shake, drop marks, staggered rise, hidden while the screen is captured); drop (countdown ring, a breathing open state, codename cards arriving as frosted glass and turning over one by one with a shimmer in New York italic); chats list (drop banner, veil orbs, 4 s polling); chat (optimistic send, polling, give-away warning sheet, reveal pill, menu with Guess who, Report, Leave and Block, each confirmed); reveal sheet (Waiting breathes and never shows anything about their tap); reveal screen (the veil lifts from the center over 1.2 s, the name typesets in 40 ms apart, two photos settle side by side, heartbeat haptic); compact Guess Who and Report.
- **LiveBlindService** (`FeatureBlind/Live/`): every `/api/blind/*` call as JSON through `GlowAPI.send`, so the bearer token only ever goes to the API host. Relative photo links (`/api/blind/cards/{id}/image`, `/api/blind/chats/{id}/photos/{n}`) load through the API and are never cached. Paths and shapes match X1's committed `contracts/openapi.yaml`.

## Ran

- `scripts/sim.sh build`: passes (Swift 6 strict concurrency, warnings as errors).
- Headless Simulator `Glow A (X2)` with `-GlowPreviewSession ready -GlowDebugRoute <route> -GlowDebugBlind <scenario>`: 8 screenshots, each one looked at: `docs/shots/x2-blind-{optin,deck,cap,drop-ready,drop-opened,chats,chat,reveal-screen}.png`. Simulator released.
